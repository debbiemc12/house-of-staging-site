Process

From first photo to closing day

Agents send room photos with rough measurements. We handle the rest and keep the seller out of the logistics.

  1. Day 0

    Send photos

    Four to eight room photos plus rough measurements or a floor plan.

  2. Within 24 hrs

    Fixed quote

    A Prep Report with the recommended package and a set price.

  3. Install day

    Prep and stage

    Punch-list work first, then furniture, art and accessories.

  4. Before listing

    Photograph

    Listing photos and matched before and after pairs from the same angles.

  5. At closing

    De-stage or buy out

    We collect the pieces, or the buyer keeps the room on a separate bill of sale.

Where it counts

The rooms we stage first

Our own inventory is chosen for these rooms. Scale, light and a clear path through the space matter more than decoration.

Living
Sets the price

Sofa scaled to the room, a rug that anchors it, one strong piece of art.

Primary bedroom
Sells the calm

Layered bedding, matched lamps, nightstands clear of everything personal.

Dining
Shows the scale

A table sized for the room so buyers see it seats six, not that it barely fits four.

Kitchen
Styled, not filled

Clear counters, three considered objects, fresh towels and good bulbs.

Beyond the install

Media and furniture buy-outs

Listing media

Photography that matches the staging

Listing photos, video and twilight shoots, plus matched before and after pairs from marked tripod positions. Available to agents who don't stage with us, too.

Buy-out

Buyers can keep the look

Buyers who fall for a staged room can buy that room's furniture, or the whole home's, on a separate bill of sale offered through the listing agent. It stays in place for showings and is handed over at closing.

Debbie McAuley founded The House of Staging & Design in Hackensack after years managing residential renovations for clients across New York City. That background is why the studio can take a listing from scuffed walls to finished photos without the agent coordinating three trades.
